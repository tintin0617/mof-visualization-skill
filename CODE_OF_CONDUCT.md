# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically honest community. Participation must remain free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and deliberate misrepresentation of data or sources.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Credit scientific data, software, and ideas.
- Distinguish verified structure facts from assumptions.
- Protect confidential and unpublished material.
- Accept correction and focus criticism on the work.

Harassment, threats, publishing private information, knowingly submitting structures without permission, or presenting fabricated structural claims as validated results are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/tintin0617/mof-visualization-skill/security/advisories/new). The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
