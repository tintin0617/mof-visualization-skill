# CIF source and preparation policy

Use sources in this order: original-paper supporting information; author or institution repository; a traceable crystallographic database; another repository only when it exposes a stable structure identifier and literature citation.

Keep the acquired source file byte-for-byte. Record its repository-relative path, SHA-256, citation, acquisition date, and structure-selection reason.

The prepared structure may omit ordinary hydrogen and explicitly identified free solvent or guests. Preserve every framework heavy atom. Record every omitted atom individually. Stop on unresolved disorder, conflicting variants, unsupported atom deletion, or an element pair whose connectivity cannot be evaluated reliably.

For every displayed metal node, retain all inorganic coordination atoms present in the CIF. Display the metal-to-coordination-atom bonds and the coordination-atom-to-organic-linker path continuously. When extracting a finite pore cage, select complete metal clusters and complete linkers; never create a boundary by deleting individual atoms from either structural unit.

The visualization represents the crystallographic structure. It does not establish defects, activation state, particle morphology, or commercial formulation.
